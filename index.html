What you can automate

Where is your team losing time?

Copying information between systems. Searching through documents. Chasing updates. Preparing the same reports each week.

These tasks keep the business moving, but they can take people away from customers and higher-value work. We help you find the right opportunities for automation and work out where AI can add value.

Here are examples of possible solutions for your business.

Customer enquiries

Capture incoming enquiries, identify what is needed and prepare a response for your team to review.

Documents and admin

Extract information from forms and supplier documents, flag missing details and send it to the right workflow.

Internal knowledge

Help staff find answers in approved policies, procedures and product information, with links to the source.

Sales and quoting

Turn enquiry details into a draft brief or quote for approval, then manage follow-up reminders.

Reporting

Bring information from different systems into a regular report without rebuilding spreadsheets each week.

Start with one process. Measure the difference. Expand where it makes sense.

How we work

Start with a clear problem and a manageable first step.

1. Understand the work

We look at the process, who uses it, the systems involved and where time is being lost. Together, we choose a useful starting point and define how to measure success.

2. Agree the approach

You receive a proposed scope, estimated costs, dependencies and ongoing running costs. We explain the options in plain language so you can make an informed decision.

3. Build and test

We build a focused first version and test it with realistic examples. Your team gets to try it and provide feedback before a wider rollout.

4. Put it to work

We help introduce the solution, document how it works and agree the support it needs. Further improvements follow the results.

Frequently asked questions

We’re interested in AI, but don’t know where to start.

Start with a task that takes too much time. We can help assess whether AI, automation or a simpler process change is worth exploring.

Do we need to replace our current software?

Often, the starting point is connecting or extending what you already use. We assess what is possible before recommending new software.

Can AI make mistakes?

Yes. We design around its limitations, test realistic examples and include human review where accuracy or consequences make it necessary.

How much will it cost?

That depends on the process, integrations and level of support required. We explain the proposed delivery costs and expected ongoing costs before implementation begins.
